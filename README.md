On [my page](https://jxd.cz/jxd-ai-snail-benchmark/) I test the capabilities of AI models in their free versions. To a free LLM model I’ll enter the prompt below and publish the results here. If you know of a free model that isn’t on the list, please send me a link to it at jxd@jxd.cz.

## Test Principle: 

The LLM being tested receives this prompt—in English, which is the native language of Euro-Atlantic LLM models—and I will publish and subjectively rate the result using a penguin icon.

# Prompt
_Create a web page (HTML) that will be included as a subpage at https://jxd.cz/
The page will feature an animation depicting the growth of a ammonite shell, from a single point to a fully grown shell. The shell’s growth from the first point to the last will take 180 seconds; then the animation will pause for 5 seconds before a new growth cycle begins.
The background will be black, with a faint gray grid in millimeters. The shell will be bright red. Above the animation will be the text “Shell Growth Model” in H2 size, in red Courier New Bold font.
At the bottom will be H3 text:
More:
and below it, two active links:_

https://demonstrations.wolfram.com/ModelOfShellGrowth/ 
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6452709/
